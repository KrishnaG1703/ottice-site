the problem

some keys only show up once.

you click create. a key flashes on screen, and the site promises you'll never see it again.

copy half of it, close the tab, get pulled into a meeting — and it's gone. back to settings, revoke, start over.

otter waits for exactly that moment.

kept close

encrypted before it ever sleeps.

  • aes-256-gcm, on your device. the key comes from your passphrase — 600,000 rounds of pbkdf2 — and the passphrase is never stored.
  • nothing readable at rest. labels, usernames and websites are encrypted too, not just the secret.
  • the exact site, or nothing. otter fills only where a key was saved: same protocol, host and port. never lookalikes, never plain http.
  • it locks itself. after 5, 15 or 30 quiet minutes. you pick.
  • only when you say so. nothing is saved until you tap keep it safe.

stays home

no servers. no accounts. no tracking.

your vault lives in your browser and nowhere else. otter doesn't phone home, doesn't count your clicks, and doesn't know who you are.
